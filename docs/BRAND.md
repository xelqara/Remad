# Brand System

## Identity

**Xelqara AI** is presented as precise, ambitious, and technologically mature. The visual system should feel engineered rather than decorative.

## Core Mark

The Xelqara mark is an angular X built from two directional forms. It represents convergence: intelligence moving from separate signals toward a coherent system. The mark should remain legible at small sizes and should not be redrawn, stretched, outlined, rotated, or recoloured outside the approved palette.

## Palette

| Token | Use | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Graphite | Primary background | `#070711` |
| Electric Violet | Intelligence, motion, emphasis | `#5B2EFF` |
| Signal Magenta | Energy, contrast, activation | `#F20D5D` |
| Ice White | Primary type | `#F6F7FB` |
| Slate | Secondary type | `#A7ADBF` |

## Typography

Use a clean geometric sans-serif for the wordmark and headings. Body copy should use a highly legible sans-serif with generous line spacing. Typography should create hierarchy through scale, weight, and spacing rather than excessive effects.

## Motion Direction

Motion should be intentional and connected. A line, particle field, or central core may carry the viewer from one scene to the next. Avoid random cuts, decorative glitches, or effects that do not communicate structure, intelligence, or progress.

## Asset Rules

The official logo asset must be stored with provenance and version information. Any generated or third-party visual must be recorded in the dependency inventory before commercial publication.
