# Intellectual Property Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how Remad records, protects, and releases intellectual property. It is designed to prevent accidental disclosure and to give investors, contributors, and partners a clear view of the intended ownership model.

## Ownership Categories

| Category | Default treatment |
|---|---|
| Xelqara-original | Proprietary to Xelqara AI only when authorship, assignment, and third-party clearance are documented. |
| Contributor-created | Controlled only according to a signed agreement or other legally valid arrangement. |
| Third-party | Governed by the original license or contract; no broader right may be claimed. |
| Public-domain or permissive | Usable only after provenance and license compatibility are recorded. |
| Confidential partner material | Restricted to the scope and duration authorized by the partner. |

## Required Records

Every material contribution should have a creator, date, source or provenance, dependency list, intended license, review status, and ownership record. A file must not be labelled “owned by Xelqara AI” merely because it is stored in a Xelqara repository.

## Brand and Trademarks

The names **Xelqara**, **Xelqara AI**, **Xelqara Labs**, **Remad**, the Xelqara logo, and related marks are intended brand assets. Registration, availability, and enforceability must be verified separately in each relevant jurisdiction before public claims or enforcement.

## Confidential Information

Do not commit secrets, private keys, credentials, unreleased customer data, personal data, confidential partner information, or unannounced research. Use secure storage and access controls instead of repository files.

## Commercial Release Gate

Before a component is sold, licensed, published, or shown to investors as owned technology, Xelqara AI should complete an ownership review, dependency review, security review, privacy review, and legal review. The release record should identify the exact artifact and the exact permission granted.

## Investor and Partner Diligence

A future diligence package should include contributor agreements, employment or contractor terms, invention assignments where applicable, dependency inventories, model and dataset licenses, trademark searches, patent strategy, and records of any confidential-information obligations.

## Draft Notice

This policy is a working internal draft and not formal legal advice. A qualified intellectual-property attorney should review it before public release, licensing, investment diligence, or enforcement.
